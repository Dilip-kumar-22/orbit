01 / About

A template that reads like a story, not a slideshow.

Most "3D websites" are a single hero animation bolted onto a flat page. ORBIT treats the whole scroll as one directed sequence: the camera drifts forward, the central form breathes and morphs, and the particle field parallaxes as your text panels arrive in rhythm.

Everything you are reading is real, selectable, scrollable text - not baked into a canvas. The 3D lives behind the content, so the page stays accessible, crawlable, and readable on a phone with a dead battery. Replace this copy with your own; the scene keeps its choreography.

03 / Capabilities

Built for craft and speed.

  • Real 3D, behind real text. A custom curl-noise core, a particle galaxy, bloom and a film grade - layered under selectable HTML.
  • Smooth without hijacking. Native scroll drives an eased camera, so it feels buttery and stays fully accessible.
  • Accessible by default. Semantic landmarks, keyboard focus, AA contrast, and a complete prefers-reduced-motion path.
  • Fast. One dependency (three.js), capped DPR, and the render loop pauses when the tab or canvas is hidden.
